# What a board's trail, foam and spray look like (from reference footage)

Notes from real surf photos and video, to steer the game's water effects. The photos themselves
are not kept here.

## Trail on the water
- Two **thin, crisp, bright lines**, one off each rail. They start right where the rail leaves the
  water, stay narrow and close together, open out only slowly, and run a long way back.
- Further back each line **frays**: it breaks into pieces, then lace, then scattered specks.
- **Between the lines** the water is darker and smoother than the sea either side: the slick the
  board leaves.
- On the **outside of a hard turn** the line becomes a **broad, churned white arc**: lacy edges,
  streaks through it, specks around it. From the shore it reads as a curved band of foam.
- Foam is streaky **along the direction of travel**, never round blots.

## Spray
- A hard turn throws a **dense white mass** off the tail: thick, bright and lumpy, like cloud.
- Round its edges, **fine round droplets** scattered against the sky: dots, not streaks or sparks.
- In motion it is a **soft, see-through white sheet** blurred along its flight, not separate blobs.
- It falls back into the water and becomes part of the churned arc on the outside of the turn.

## What did not work (tried and removed)
- Separate puff sprites lying on the water: read as round blobs.
- Long stretched drop particles and over-bright glints: read as sparks.
- Mist and a foam ring drawn on the board's side: read as a white smear on the board.
